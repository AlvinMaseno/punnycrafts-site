# Admin features, ranked

Already prototyped: log orders from social media, stock counts, replenish level, restock requests, order status steps.

## Build early (daily use)
1. Order board with status columns and stuck-order flags
2. Payment tracking with M-Pesa codes, unpaid and deposit filters
3. Customer records built from orders, with order history per customer
4. Stock movement history, so any count can be explained
5. Product manager: edit price, photos, replenish level, active or hidden
6. Roles: admin sees everything, staff cannot change prices or delete

## Build next (saves money and mistakes)
7. Cost and margin per product, using the costs in your product guide (printing, lamination, cutting, purchase price)
8. Production jobs: when a restock is requested for paper goods, calculate A3 sheets needed from the guide's yields (for example 28 square magnets per A3 sheet, 18 Sunny Rays per sheet)
9. Supplier list with the vendors in your guide and what each supplies
10. Low-stock digest sent to you each morning by email or WhatsApp
11. Delivery fees by area, and a delivery run sheet for the day
12. Custom order intake: customer text or artwork reference, design approval step
13. Wholesale and bulk quote builder

## Later
14. Daily and monthly sales by channel (Instagram vs WhatsApp vs walk-in) and by product
15. Expense log and simple profit report
16. Discount codes and gift bundles
17. Audit log of who changed what
18. Connect your existing CRM and WhatsApp broadcast tools so customers flow in automatically
