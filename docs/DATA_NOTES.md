## Fulfilment labels

The tags "Ready to ship", "Made to order" and "Made for you" come from the guide's fulfilment table. Please confirm them, especially wooden keyholders (the guide lists 5 designs in stock but also says all keyholders are made to order) and drinkware (made to order, with 2 to 3 seasonal designs held for display).

## Left out on purpose

Production costs, vendor names, vendor phone numbers and process steps are internal. They are not in `data/products.ts` and must not be added to any page. They belong in the admin area in a later phase.
